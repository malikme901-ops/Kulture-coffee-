Born in Kolkata

The “K” in Kulture is a nod to Kolkata. Climb the old wooden stairs, step into a bright, colourful space and sip coffee like you are inside a vintage postcard.

More than a café

Kulture is a place to meet, work, learn and create.

  • Co-working friendly – a calm corner with a view to get things done.
  • Art exhibits – the walls make room for local stories and artists.
  • Coffee workshops – craft your own cup with a barista guiding you.
  • Private events – bring your people together; the team takes care of the rest.
